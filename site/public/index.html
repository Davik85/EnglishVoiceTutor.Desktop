Frequently Asked Questions About AI Language Practice

Learn how Language Voice Tutor supports speaking practice, corrections, language levels, and realistic AI conversations.

Can I practice speaking with an AI language tutor?

Yes. You can practice speaking by voice, type your answers, or combine both formats. The AI tutor follows the selected topic, responds to your answers, and provides corrections based on your CEFR level.

Is Language Voice Tutor suitable for beginners?

Yes. Choose level A1 for short, simple conversations, then move through A2, B1, and B2 as your confidence and vocabulary improve.

Which languages can I practice?

You can practice English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese at CEFR levels A1, A2, B1, and B2.

Does the AI tutor correct grammar and phrases?

Yes. When a phrase can be improved, the tutor suggests a clearer or more natural version and helps you repeat it before continuing.

Can I use both voice and text chat?

Yes. Speak aloud, type your answers, or switch between voice and text during the same language lesson.
